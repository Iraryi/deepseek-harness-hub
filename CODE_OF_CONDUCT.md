# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, practical, and harassment-free project for contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent, criticize implementations rather than people, disclose conflicts of interest, and avoid publishing private information. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
