DSHMK / dsh-plugins-store
-------------------------
The bundled dshmk-catalog.json snapshot is derived from the public DSHMK
catalog and the dsh-plugins-store project maintained by ZASENJC and its
contributors. The dsh-plugins-store source is distributed under the MIT
License. Live metadata, validation results, and installation references remain
maintained by their upstream contributors.

DSH HUB independently validates registry identity, source provenance, immutable
versions, download hosts, hashes, licenses, permissions, and install behavior.
